核心能力 / Core capabilities

01 / 3D SCENES

3D 场景生成
3D scene generation

将空间、结构与过程转化为立体场景，让难以直接观察的知识更容易被看见和理解。

Visualize spaces, structures, and processes in 3D so abstract or hard-to-observe concepts become easier to understand.

02 / DIGITAL HUMAN

数字人讲解场景
Digital human presenters

生成由数字人出镜讲解的教学场景，为不同课程内容提供自然、清晰的叙事载体。

Create presenter-led educational scenes with digital humans for clear, natural explanations across different subjects.

03 / LEARNING SCENARIOS

多样教育场景
Diverse learning scenarios

根据学科、知识点与表达目标组织多种视觉场景，为教育视频提供更灵活的呈现方式。

Compose varied visual settings around subjects, concepts, and teaching goals for more flexible educational storytelling.
