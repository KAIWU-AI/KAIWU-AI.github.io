AgentV，让知识 成为生动的视频。

Turn knowledge into vivid educational videos.

AgentV 是面向教育领域的 AI 视频生成产品。输入教学主题与表达意图，即可构建包含 3D 场景、数字人讲解与多样化视觉场景的教育视频。

AgentV is an AI video generation product for education. Turn a teaching topic into a video with 3D environments, digital human presenters, and diverse visual learning scenarios.
